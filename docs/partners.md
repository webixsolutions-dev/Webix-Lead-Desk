# Partners, targeting and results

Use a separate Webix Titan mailbox profile with lane `partner`; Camzio Tech client conversations
keep their original client sender. Gmail/Googlemail profiles are not eligible. Existing company
conversation locks prevent contacting the same business again through another campaign or lane.

On the business record, the owner reviews Canada/US location evidence and the sender/recipient
requirements, including cross-border rules. Reviews expire after 30 days and are rechecked at
approval and dispatch. The existing contact-basis, suppression and exact-message controls remain.
The country label is not a legal exemption or a determination that outreach is lawful.

A shared Calgary-day counter permits five new partner businesses across every campaign.
A business consumes one slot before manual draft creation or any optional AI call. Revisions
reuse its slot. Failed model attempts retain their reservation conservatively. This does not
authorize spending or sending. Partner email campaign caps are separately limited to five.

Campaign targeting is editable in Partners & Results. Version checks prevent lost edits; historical
import snapshots and conversation routing remain unchanged. Results count known email acceptances,
non-automatic/non-bounce reply conversations, current qualified stages and owner-recorded won
CAD values. These numbers are not inbox-placement estimates, causal attribution or cash receipts.

## Manual contact forms

Prepare personal copy on a verified business website, record why the form permits this relevant
enquiry, and approve the exact revision, sender and URL. Approval reserves the form channel across
campaigns; email first touch is then blocked. Existing email reservation or any recorded recipient
suppression blocks form preparation/approval. The current page rechecks these conditions before
showing the approved form link. The application never fills, submits or bypasses a third-party form
or CAPTCHA. The owner performs one manual submission and records its real outcome. Uncertain
outcomes retain the channel reservation and must not be repeated. Outcome recording preserves
history even if a recipient opt-out arrived after the owner had already submitted.

## Reference checks

- FTC CAN-SPAM business guide: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business
- CRTC implied-consent guidance: https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/guide.htm
- CRTC publication questions: https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm

The FTC guide covers commercial B2B email too. The CRTC guidance requires consent, identification
and an unsubscribe mechanism; publication needs a specific relevance/restriction assessment.
These sources support the separate owner review, not a blanket permission to contact scraped leads.
