# Meeting preparation and proposal review

Bookings snapshot a bounded preparation brief. Only an exact contact email match imports an email opening and latest reply; names and company similarity never merge histories. Industry questions remain hypotheses. Historical bookings can prepare a missing snapshot with an explicit owner action. Packs are private and printable; a booking is not external calendar availability.

An actual past confirmed booking can be completed. Owner-confirmed notes are immutable revisions. The editable proposal structure covers summary, scope, exclusions, delivery dependencies, handover/support and commercial terms. The referenced owner template was not provided, so these are neutral editable sections, not a claim to reproduce an unseen template. Price questions can still be answered through reviewed email/LinkedIn replies without requiring a meeting.

Only an owner enters exact prices and currency. Blank means unquoted; explicit zero is preserved. Approval binds recipient, sender identity, notes, booking version, sections, prices and bounded conversation fingerprint. A new revision supersedes earlier unsent versions. Changed context blocks approval, current export and recording delivery. The Word file contains editable text, the actual recorded brand/name/contact, and no remote logo, macros or private notes beyond the owner-selected sections. A downloaded edit must be copied back and approved as a new revision before delivery.

Export and approval do not send. The owner records actual manual delivery of the exact approved document with timestamp and reference. This creates day 2, 5 and 9 review reminders, not outgoing jobs. New replies, stopped/held conversations, closed deals and replacement proposals hold the old sequence. Any email or LinkedIn response still goes through its own exact-content approval. External calendars and automatic LinkedIn chat synchronization remain unavailable.

Completed meetings advance a matching email card to Meeting held; actual recorded delivery advances it to Proposal sent, then human review can move to Negotiation and Won/Lost with reasons. Older Call held/Proposal records remain readable without inventing delivery history. LinkedIn cards retain manual attributable stage recording.
