How these amounts are calculated

All amounts are USD, rounded to cents. This is the app's ledger, not a live provider balance. Viewing this page does not run AI or change reservations.

Today and monthly caps follow Calgary time. The estimate uses this month's average confirmed spend; it is not a forecast of provider charges.

Ratios divide all confirmed monthly AI spend by {{ ai_usage.lead_count }} businesses with attributed AI calls or {{ ai_usage.approval_count }} approved email revisions. They are not incremental task prices; — means no denominator yet.
